Privacy Policy

FoodE · Effective date: 16 October 2026

This policy explains how FoodE handles information when you use the FoodE food-ordering service for the SRM University–AP community. It applies to the FoodE mobile app and website. FoodE is not an official university service unless separately authorized.

Privacy contact: foodeforus@gmail.com. You can use this address for privacy questions, correction requests, and account deletion requests.

Information FoodE handles

  • Account information: name, mobile number, FoodE password (stored as a protected hash), profile image if provided, account role and status.
  • Student eligibility information: information returned during SRM University–AP ERP verification, which can include registration number, student name, institution, program, section, semester, specialization, date of birth, gender, and email. FoodE sends the student portal credentials you enter to the university ERP service to perform that verification and does not save those credentials in the FoodE account.
  • Orders and delivery: selected items, quantities, prices, order history, delivery name, phone number, address, and any note you add to an order.
  • Other account activity: saved restaurants or foods, ratings, reviews, review comments, and notification records associated with your use of FoodE.
  • Payment records: payment method, amount, and status needed to record and support an order. FoodE's current payment records do not include card or bank credentials.
  • App and service information: profile image if provided, push-notification token, and support correspondence you send us.
  • Restaurant partner information: for partner accounts, restaurant name, description, contact details, address, menu and operating information, and uploaded restaurant or menu images.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify student eligibility, provide customer and restaurant features, take and fulfil orders, coordinate delivery, record and reconcile payments, send service notifications, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, and maintain and improve FoodE.

How information is shared

We share order details needed to prepare and deliver an order with the restaurant and delivery personnel involved. FoodE uses Google Firebase for push notifications and Cloudinary to store uploaded images; these providers receive the information needed to provide those services. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users, the service, or legal rights. FoodE does not sell personal information as a business practice.

Security

FoodE uses reasonable safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Account passwords are stored as hashes rather than plain text.

Retention and deletion

One-time student verification records expire after 10 minutes. We keep other information while it is needed to provide FoodE, maintain order and payment records, handle support or disputes, protect the service, and meet legal obligations. When an account deletion request is completed, we delete or de-identify account information and associated data that we no longer need. Some records may be retained where required for legal, accounting, payment, security, or fraud-prevention purposes; access to retained records is limited and they are deleted when no longer required.

To request deletion, visit FoodE Account Deletion or email foodeforus@gmail.com from the address below. We may ask for information needed to verify account ownership. Do not send your password, one-time codes, or payment credentials.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information associated with your account. You can manage push-notification permissions in your device settings; turning them off may prevent you from receiving order updates as push notifications.

Children

FoodE is intended for the SRM University–AP community and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to FoodE, contact us so we can review it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when FoodE or its data practices change. The latest version and effective date will be published on this page.
