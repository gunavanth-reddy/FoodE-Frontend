Account and data deletion

Delete your FoodE account

You can request deletion of your FoodE account and associated personal data without signing in or reinstalling the app.

Contact FoodE Support
Send your request to foodeforus@gmail.com
Email a deletion request

What to include

  1. Use the mobile number registered to your FoodE account and the name on the account so support can locate and verify it.
  2. Write that you are requesting deletion of your FoodE account and associated personal data.
  3. Do not include your password, one-time codes, or payment card/bank credentials.

What happens next

FoodE Support will review the request and may contact you to verify account ownership. After verification, FoodE will delete or de-identify the account and associated personal data that is not required to be retained. Certain order, payment, security, or legal records may need to be retained for applicable legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes; retained information will be restricted and deleted when it is no longer required.

If you cannot open an email app, send a message manually to foodeforus@gmail.com with the subject FoodE Account Deletion Request.

For more information about information FoodE handles and its retention practices, read the FoodE Privacy Policy.
